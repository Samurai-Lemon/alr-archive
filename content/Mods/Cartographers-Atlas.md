## About

Cartographer's Atlas draws the world you've explored as an isometric 3D model on a sheet of parchment. It remembers every place you've been, so the map fills in as you travel, and it all happens on your own computer: it works on any server.

## Features

- **3D world map:** open it with a key and see the terrain you've explored as a small 3D model, textured block by block or in the flat colors of a vanilla map.
- **Cave maps:** underground, the map shows the caves around you instead of the surface. The Nether is shown as a slice at your height.
- **Waypoints:** right-click the map, or press **B** in the world, to add one. Name and color them, hide them, and see them floating in the world, on the compass, or both.
- **Death waypoints:** marks where you die.
- **Compass:** a compass across the top of the screen, shown always or only while you carry one. A recovery compass also marks where you last died.
- **Block info:** hover the map to see a block's name, position and biome.
- **Teleport:** with cheats on, click a waypoint to teleport to it.

## Installing

1. Install [Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/use/) for Minecraft 1.21.1.
2. Download the jar above and [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api).
3. Put both in your `.minecraft/mods` folder.
