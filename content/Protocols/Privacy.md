# Privacy Policy

> [!info] Data Handling Notice
> The [[ALR Initiative]] collects minimal personnel data strictly for submission processing and archive record maintenance. All data handling is administered by Archive Operations in accordance with ALR Data Retention Protocol.

---

## Information Collected

When submitting materials to [[The Archive]], the following information may be collected:

- Name or handle provided by the submitter
- Contact address for correspondence purposes
- Submitted content in its entirety

---

## Purpose of Collection

Collected information is used exclusively to:

- Review and process submissions for potential archival inclusion
- Contact submitters if follow-up correspondence is required
- Maintain accurate provenance records within [[The Archive]]

Information collected during submission is not used for any purpose outside of these functions.

---

## Data Storage

Submitted information may be stored indefinitely as part of the archival record. Once integrated into [[The Archive]], submission data is treated as a permanent archival document and is subject to the same retention standards as all other Initiative records.

---

## Data Sharing

The [[ALR Initiative]] does not sell, distribute, or otherwise transfer personal data to external parties. Information may be disclosed only under circumstances required by applicable law or by formal directive from Archive Operations.

---

## Third-Party Services

The [[ALR Initiative]] does not endorse or assume responsibility for data practices associated with third-party services that may be involved in submission handling or site functionality. Such services operate according to their own respective policies, which are independent of ALR Data Retention Protocol.

---

## User Rights

Submitters may request:

- Access to their submitted data held within Archive Operations records
- Clarification regarding how submitted information is stored or used

Requests should be directed to Archive Operations through established Initiative channels. Responses are not guaranteed within a fixed interval. As noted in the [[Terms-of-Submission]], archived content may not be removable following integration into the primary record.

---

## Contact

For inquiries regarding data handling, contact Archive Operations directly through established Initiative channels.
