# Privacy Policy

> [!info] Data Handling Notice
> The [[ALR Initiative]] collects minimal personnel data strictly for submission processing and archive record maintenance. All data handling is administered by Archive Operations in accordance with ALR Data Retention Protocol.

---

## Information Collected

When submitting materials to [[The Archive]], the following information may be collected:

- Name or handle provided by the submitter
- Contact address for correspondence purposes
- Submitted content in its entirety

---

## Purpose of Collection

Collected information is used exclusively to:

- Review and process submissions for potential archival inclusion
- Contact submitters if follow-up correspondence is required
- Maintain accurate provenance records within [[The Archive]]

Information collected during submission is not used for any purpose outside of these functions.

---

## Data Storage

Submitted information may be stored indefinitely as part of the archival record. Once integrated into [[The Archive]], submission data is treated as a permanent archival document and is subject to the same retention standards as all other Initiative records.

---

## Data Sharing

The [[ALR Initiative]] does not sell, distribute, or otherwise transfer personal data to external parties. Information may be disclosed only under circumstances required by applicable law or by formal directive from Archive Operations. Advertising and account services are described in the real-world notice below.

---

## Third-Party Services

The [[ALR Initiative]] does not endorse or assume responsibility for data practices associated with third-party services that may be involved in submission handling or site functionality. Such services operate according to their own respective policies, which are independent of ALR Data Retention Protocol.

---

## Advertising & Cookies (Real-World Notice)

> [!warning] Out-of-character notice
> This section is not part of the fiction. It describes how this website actually handles visitor data.

**Advertising.** This site uses Google AdSense to show ads, labelled "Sponsored • External Signal".

- Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website and other websites.
- Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
- You can opt out of personalized advertising in [Google's Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/).
- You can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).
- Learn more in [How Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

**Browser storage.** This site saves a few settings in your browser's local storage: your light or dark theme, whether sound is on, and the state of the sidebar. These stay on your device and are not sent anywhere.

**Accounts.** If you create an account, your email address and password are handled by [Supabase](https://supabase.com/privacy), our account and database provider, and are used only to sign you in and link you to your submissions. Passwords are never visible to us.

**Shop.** Product listings are loaded from our online store. Purchases are completed on the store's own checkout, which handles payment and shipping details under its own privacy policy. This site never sees your payment information.

**Your choices.** You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can ask for your account and submissions to be deleted at any time using the contact below.

**Contact.** For any real-world privacy question or deletion request, email [contact@alrinitiative.com](mailto:contact@alrinitiative.com).

## User Rights

Submitters may request:

- Access to their submitted data held within Archive Operations records
- Clarification regarding how submitted information is stored or used

Requests should be directed to Archive Operations through established Initiative channels. Responses are not guaranteed within a fixed interval. As noted in the [[Terms-of-Submission]], archived content may not be removable following integration into the primary record.
