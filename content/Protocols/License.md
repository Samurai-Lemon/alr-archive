## ALR-Specific Clause

All derivative works produced from [[The Archive]] must additionally preserve:

- Narrative integrity — the internal consistency and established lore of the ALR universe
- Classification structure — the use of established ALR classification systems including RTS, RDS, RCC, EC, and ESC designations

Derivative works that materially misrepresent the ALR classification record, contradict formally established Archive entries, or reproduce restricted content without authorization are considered outside the scope of this license regardless of attribution.

Certain entries within [[The Archive]] may be subject to licensing restrictions beyond those described above. These include entries containing:

- Restricted materials designated for limited personnel access

Content falling under these exceptions may not be freely reusable under the standard license. Queries regarding the licensing status of restricted entries should be directed to Archive Operations.
