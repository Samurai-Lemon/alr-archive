# Terms of Submission

> [!info] Archive Policy
> All submissions to the [[ALR Initiative]] are subject to review, modification, and archival control by Archive Operations.

---

## Submission Ownership

By submitting content to the [[ALR Initiative]], you affirm that:

- You are the original creator of the submitted material, or have the right to submit it on behalf of its creator
- The submission does not infringe upon any third-party rights or restricted classifications maintained by [[The Archive]]

---

## License Grant

By submitting, you grant the [[ALR Initiative]] a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide license to use, modify, adapt, publish, archive, and distribute your submission in any format or medium.

This includes incorporation into [[The Archive]] and all related Initiative materials.

---

## Attribution

Submissions may be:

- Credited to the provided name or handle
- Published anonymously at the submitter's request
- Edited for formatting, clarity, or classification consistency with Archive standards

---

## Content Modification

The [[ALR Initiative]] reserves the right to:

- Edit submissions for consistency with established Archive documentation standards
- Reclassify submitted entries under the appropriate classification system
- Integrate submissions into existing records where overlap or cross-reference is identified

---

## Removal Policy

Submitted content may not be removable after archival integration. Requests for removal may be reviewed by Archive Operations but are not guaranteed consideration. Once a submission has been incorporated into the primary record, its status as an archival document supersedes the submitter's right to withdrawal.

---

## Prohibited Content

The following categories of submission are not accepted under any circumstances:

- Illegal content of any kind
- Copyrighted material the submitter does not own or have rights to submit
- Malicious, harmful, or deliberately falsified documentation

Submissions suspected of containing fabricated anomalous data will be flagged for review by the Echo Research Division before any archival action is taken.

---

## Acceptance

Submission does not guarantee inclusion in [[The Archive]]. All entries are subject to review by Archive Operations personnel before formal registration. Submissions that do not meet Archive documentation standards will be returned with a classification recommendation or rejected without further correspondence.

---

For inquiries regarding submissions, contact Archive Operations directly through established Initiative channels.
