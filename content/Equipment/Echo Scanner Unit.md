# ECHO Scanner Unit

> [!info] Device Classification
> **Device Type:** Echo Detection Instrument
> **Primary Users:** Reality Investigation Division, Echo Research Division
> **Operational Authority:** [[ALR Initiative]]

---
![[ECHO Scanner Unit.png]]
## Development

The ECHO Scanner Unit is a device used by personnel to locate, analyze, and track anomalous phenomena classified as [[ECHOES]]. It serves as the primary investigative instrument used during field operations conducted by the [[ALR Initiative]].

The Echo Scanner Unit was created during the earliest period of activity within [[The Archive]]. At the time, personnel had no reliable method of identifying or studying Echo phenomena within collapsed realities. The scanner was developed to detect the unusual energy patterns associated with Echoes and provide a consistent method for recording their properties. The device quickly became one of the most essential tools used during exploration missions.

---

## Function

The ECHO Scanner Unit performs several core functions during investigations:

- detecting the presence of Echo phenomena
- analyzing anomalous structures
- recording Echo classification data
- identifying connections to collapsed realities
- logging investigation data for archival storage

The scanner allows personnel to gather the information required to document Echoes using the [[Echo Classification (EC)]] and [[Echo Stability Classification (ESC)]] systems.

---

## Operation

### ECHO Detection

Echoes emit detectable irregularities that differ from the surrounding structure of a reality. The scanner is capable of identifying these irregularities and isolating the location of an Echo phenomenon. Once detected, the device can perform localized analysis to assist investigators in documenting the anomaly.

### Reality Navigation

In addition to detecting Echoes, the scanner can assist personnel in identifying points of transition between realities. These transition points allow investigators to move between locations connected to [[The Archive]] during investigation missions. Because of this capability, the Echo Scanner Unit also serves as a navigation tool during exploration of collapsed realities.

---

## Notes

> [!note]- Commentary

The Echo Scanner Unit is considered standard equipment for personnel conducting field investigations. Most Echo discoveries recorded by the [[ALR Initiative]] have been made using this device. Without it, identifying Echo phenomena within unfamiliar environments would be significantly more difficult.
